# Prepared price event schedule — 15 November 2026

Production deployment `6af99d5394048d41bbfbc908` and the two prepared-price migrations introduce event-led refresh. Published/legacy price and manual price changes use existing brand-scoped source revision events. Customer pricing and access events remain connected. A successful or source-unavailable profile schedules its next check at the earliest future price boundary or seven days; other errors retain a 15-minute retry. UTC price start and inclusive end dates retain their existing semantics. A changed revision during a successful or source-unavailable claim is queued again. The existing global lease and single active cold export remain authoritative.

The scheduled worker now checks up to eight profiles within a 12-second start budget and stops on a cold build. The underlying Netlify scheduled function has a 30-second limit; a request already started may exceed the start budget and is still protected by the existing lease. Brands having only an effective active manual price and an enabled supplier are now admitted by the priced-brand check.

Validation: build and 19 relevant prewarm, SQL, manual-price and recovery tests passed; staging migrations, boundary query and access restrictions verified. The prior suite had one intermittent cache-receipt test failure in an earlier run, followed by passing runs; no claim of resolving that independent test timing issue is made. Production `/version.json` confirmed the deploy. In its first observed tick, eight profiles were checked and overdue profiles fell from 61 to 54 (new due times can change while observing). One older profile had already adopted the weekly schedule before deployment. Five active manual-price records and 31 publication pointers had identical before/after digests. No customer order or price list was modified by this work.

Source-unavailable profiles still require actual published or manual prices; the schedule does not invent or cross-map prices. End-to-end fresh workbook generation for every profile was not tested.
